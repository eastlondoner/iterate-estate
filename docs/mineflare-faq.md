# Mineflare FAQs

Q: What is Mineflare?
A: Mineflare is a Minecraft server hosting service that runs on Cloudflare.

Q: How much does Mineflare cost?
A: It costs $0.50 per hour that your Minecraft server is running.

Q: What if I forget to turn off my Mineflare server?
A: Minecraft servers automatically shut down after 20 minutes of inactivity.
