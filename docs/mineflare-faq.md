# Mineflare FAQ

## What is Mineflare?
Mineflare is a Minecraft server hosting service that runs on Cloudflare.

## How much does Mineflare cost?
It costs $0.50 per hour that your Minecraft server is running.

## What if I forget to turn off my Mineflare server?
Minecraft servers automatically shut down after 20 minutes of inactivity.
